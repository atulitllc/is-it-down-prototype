Error 500 Internal Server Error: What It Means

An HTTP 500 error means the server hit an unexpected problem while handling your request. It is almost always on the service side, not your device.

  1. Wait two minutes, then reload the page.
  2. Check the service status page here or the official status page.
  3. Sign out and back in, or try a private window.
  4. If it keeps happening, contact the service through its official help channels.

How to fix Figma

  1. If Figma is frozen, close it: from the Home Screen swipe up from the bottom and pause, find the app, then swipe up on its preview. Reopen it. Source
  2. Update the app: open the App Store, tap your photo at the top, tap App Updates, then Update. Source
  1. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data connection. A strong connection, ideally Wi-Fi, is best. Source
  2. Check storage: open Settings, tap Storage. Less than 1 GB free can stop apps from updating. Source
  3. Check for Android system updates in Settings. Source
  1. Open the official Figma status page to see if the problem is on their side. Source

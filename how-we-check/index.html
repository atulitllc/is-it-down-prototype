How we check

Three signals, shown honestly.

1. Your browser

Check now times a request from your device. In this prototype it pings example.com as a demo.

2. Community reports

Reports you submit are counted per hour. Empty means nobody reported.

3. Official status

We link to each service's own status page.

What we never do

  • Show estimated or placeholder report counts.
  • Back fill outage history before tracking began.

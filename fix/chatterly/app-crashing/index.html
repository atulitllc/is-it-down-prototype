Chatterly not working? 4 fixes for App crashing (iPhone)

Tested on iPhone 15 and Chrome 129, Oct 13, 2026. Not affiliated with Chatterly. Steps ordered quick to drastic.

  1. Force close Chatterly: swipe up and hold, then swipe the app away.
  2. Turn Airplane Mode on for 10 seconds, then off.
  3. Update Chatterly in the App Store.
  4. Restart your iPhone, then delete and reinstall if it still fails.

Source: Chatterly official help center

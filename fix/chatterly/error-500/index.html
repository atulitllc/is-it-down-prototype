Chatterly error 500: what it means and how to fix it

Error 500 means Chatterly servers hit an unexpected problem. It is usually on their side. Check the official status, wait a few minutes, then retry.

  1. Reload after 2 minutes.
  2. Check Chatterly status.
  3. Sign out and back in.
  4. Contact Chatterly via official help channels only: help center.
